openSUSE Event Requests

Use this form to ask the openSUSE project to sponsor an event, back a booth or stand, or host a local meetup. The project prioritises community-run, affordable, openly accessible events — it does not fund commercial conferences that charge attendees heavily.

This form is not for personal travel funding. Travel, accommodation and individual attendance are handled separately by the travel support programme — please use that instead.

Request type *

8-week minimum notice. Requests are funded from donations to the openSUSE project. Submit this form at least 8 weeks before the event. Requests arriving later may be declined purely on timing.

Event report expected. Sponsored participants are asked to publish a short event report with photos on news.opensuse.org or the openSUSE forums within four weeks, and to hand back any unused funds or materials.

Community-first funding. openSUSE prioritises events that are affordable and open to the public. Commercial conferences with high entry fees, closed attendance, or a mainly vendor-showcase programme are unlikely to be funded.
