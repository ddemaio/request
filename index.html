openSUSE Event Requests

Ask the openSUSE project to sponsor an event, back a booth or stand, or host a local meetup. Priority goes to community-run, affordable, openly accessible events — not commercial conferences with heavy entry fees. Fill in what you can; nothing here is mandatory, and email is the best way to reach you.
